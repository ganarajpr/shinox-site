Why Shinox

It works, and it’s kind.

Exceptional hygiene is not a luxury. It is an everyday essential. Strong cleaners usually leave something harsh behind, so we hold every formula to three rules instead.

  • No artificial colouring
  • Lab tested
  • ISO certified company
  • Made in India

Work brilliantly

Every formula has to beat the grease, stains, rust or smell it was made for. Shine comes first.

Stay gentle

Tread lightly

Our Story

It started with a question at home.

Why do the cleaners that work best feel the harshest, and why do the gentle ones give up halfway?

  1. The search

    Products that could handle real Indian homes, from greasy kadais to monsoon floors, without leaving harsh chemicals behind.

  2. The first formula

    We kept testing until tough and gentle sat in the same bottle, free of artificial colouring and honest about what it does.

  3. Shinox today

    Nine specialised home-care solutions for personal care, fresh spaces, spotless floors, sparkling surfaces, clean kitchens and well-kept fabrics.

Beyond cleaning, our range extends to bamboo-based face tissues, tissue napkins, kitchen napkins, toilet rolls and garbage covers, chosen for modern living.
