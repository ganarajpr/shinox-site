It started with a question at home.

Why do the cleaners that work best feel the harshest, and why do the gentle ones give up halfway?

  1. The search

    We wanted products that could handle real Indian homes, from greasy kadais to monsoon floors, without leaving harsh chemicals behind.

  2. The first formula

  3. Shinox today

    Nine products for every room, from the kitchen and bathroom to the family silver, each made to one promise of shine you can trust.
